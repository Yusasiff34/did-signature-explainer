DID Signature Explainer

Learn what a cryptographic signature proves — and what it does not prove.

What does a signature prove?

  • Control of a key: A valid signature demonstrates access to the corresponding private key.
  • Message integrity: Changing the signed message makes verification fail.
  • Not automatically real-world identity: A signature alone does not prove who the key holder is.
